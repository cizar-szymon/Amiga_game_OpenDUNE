Put the music recordings here (DUNE*_*.RAW, 33 files, about 75 MB).
Tu wloz nagrania muzyki (DUNE*_*.RAW, 33 pliki, ok. 75 MB).

They are made once on a PC from your own game data:
Robi sie je raz na PC z wlasnych danych gry:

  python tools/music/make_music.py --data DIRECTORY_WITH_THE_PAK_FILES

(sources: github.com/cizar-szymon/Amiga_game_OpenDUNE; needs Python 3 with
numpy, ffmpeg and Docker). Without them the game runs without music.
See OpenDUNE_en.txt / OpenDUNE.txt, "MUSIC" / "MUZYKA".
